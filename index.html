THE PROBLEM

Most GTM gets built backwards.

Growth stalls, sales swing month to month, acquisition gets expensive or traction stays unclear. The usual answer is more marketing spend. The cause usually sits earlier, in five questions nobody answered before the first channel went live.

01Who should you sell to?
02What are they actually buying?
03How do you reach them?
04What happens when they check you out?
05What is the market telling you?

We answer these before we scale a single channel.

THE POSITIONING RECORD

Your GTM needs a memory.

Most GTM work forgets. A campaign runs, someone learns something, that person moves on, and the next campaign starts from the same old assumptions.

The Positioning Record is one versioned page per buyer segment. It holds what real buyers said and what we changed because of it. Every monthly cycle adds to it, so month six starts with everything months one to five taught.

SAMPLE RECORD · Waterproofing chemicals, contractor segment

ICP Waterproofing contractors on residential towers, 8 to 40 floors, Tier-2 metros

Buyer Project purchase head. Signs off up to ₹15L. The site engineer influences the spec, and the MD approves anything above ₹15L.

Trigger A structural consultant specifies a membrane system at drawing stage, four to seven months before anyone buys.

Message that works Approved by the consultant, available at the site, with a warranty the developer will accept.

Objection "Your rate is 12% above the brand we already use, and my applicator knows that product."

Competitor The incumbent wins because applicators already know its product.

Lost deal Tender, March 2026. Lost after technical approval, because the applicator refused to switch systems mid-project.

Evidence behind this Three quotation threads, one lost tender file, two dealer debriefs, one recorded site meeting.

What we changed Started selling to the applicator as well as the purchase head. Added applicator training to the offer.

Sample. Yours starts from your own deals in Week One.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is a GTM leak read?+

A free, one-page read of your last 90 days of enquiries. It shows where enquiries stopped moving and what we'd look at first. You send counts, not logins.

What is the Tuning Curve Framework?+

Our method for fixing B2B go-to-market. It maps your GTM across five layers (Who, What, Reach, Convert, Learn) to find where revenue leaks. Then it runs in monthly cycles: diagnose, prioritise, install, measure, learn.

What is a Positioning Record?+

One versioned page per buyer segment, holding what real buyers said and what changed because of it. It's updated every cycle, so each month starts from what the last one taught.

Do I have to commit for months?+

No. Nothing renews automatically: you buy Week One, decide on the build after it, and buy cycles one at a time. We recommend three cycles, since one month of outreach is too small a sample to judge, but you can stop after any of them.

How much does it cost?+

The leak read is free. Week One is a fixed fee, agreed before we start. The build and the cycles are quoted after Week One, once the scope is clear.

Can you guarantee leads?+

No. What we can commit to: within 90 days you'll know which channels bring customers who pay, and which only bring activity.

We already have an agency.+

That's common. An agency runs channels, and we fix the layer underneath: who you sell to and why they buy. When that's right, your agency gets more from the same budget.

We tried digital marketing and it didn't work.+

Usually the spend was pointed at the wrong buyers. That's the first thing we check.

Will you contact our customers?+

Only if you introduce us, and only people you choose. Most of Week One happens inside your own records and with your own sales team.

Where does AI fit in?+

AI workflows handle account research, enrichment, outbound, follow-ups and CRM updates. Diagnosis, positioning and priorities stay with people, and every recommendation traces back to your own records.

Who owns what you build?+

You do. Everything sits in your accounts and your infrastructure, under your logins.

What if you can't help us?+

We say so, at the leak read or at the end of Week One, before you spend more.

Do you work outside India?+

Yes. We work with B2B companies in India, the UAE, the US and wider Asia.
