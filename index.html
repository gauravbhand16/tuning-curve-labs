THE GTM MEMORY

Your GTM needs a memory.

The Positioning Record is the versioned source of truth behind your GTM system. Every month, new evidence updates the record. Every GTM decision downstream gets better.

ICP

Waterproofing contractors on residential towers, 8 to 40 floors, Tier-2 metros

BUYER

Project purchase head. Signs off to ₹15L. Site engineer influences the spec, MD approves above ₹15L.

TRIGGER

Structural consultant specifies a membrane system at drawing stage, four to seven months before anyone buys.

MESSAGE

Approved by the consultant, available at the site, warranty the developer will accept.

OBJECTION

“Your rate is 12% above the brand we already use, and my applicator knows that product.”

COMPETITOR

Incumbent wins on applicator familiarity rather than on product.

LOST DEAL

Tender, March 2026. Lost after technical approval. The applicator refused to switch systems mid-project.

EVIDENCE

Three quotation threads, one lost tender file, two dealer debriefs, one recorded site meeting.

WHAT WE CHANGED

Started selling to the applicator, not only the purchase head. Added applicator training to the offer.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I have to sign a retainer?

No. Nothing on our side renews automatically. You buy one cycle at a time and stop whenever you want.

Can you guarantee leads?

No. What we can commit to is that within 90 days you'll know which channels produce customers who actually pay, and which ones only produce activity.

We already have an agency.

Most of our clients do. An agency runs channels. We work on the layer underneath: who you're selling to and why they buy.

We tried digital marketing and it didn't work.

That's usually a targeting problem rather than a channel problem. The first thing we'd look at is who the spend was pointed at.

Will you contact our customers?

Only if you introduce us, and only to people you choose.

Why do most clients run three cycles?

One month of outreach is too small a sample to conclude anything from. It's a recommendation, not a minimum.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Everything is built inside your accounts and your infrastructure.

What if you don't think you can help us?

We say so at the end of Week One, before you spend anything more.
